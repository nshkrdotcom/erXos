# The files interface (docs/design-files.md, "Serving: the erx_fs behaviour"): an erx_fs
# grant of a tree, which a client mounts in its namespace and speaks 9P2000.L to. Its
# operations are the 9P protocol's own messages, which no facet row names, so it declares
# none. Every grantee and every server of a files facet carries frames of at least
# files_max_frame_min bytes (property 16): a read reply or a write request of the client's
# 64 KiB transfer unit and its encoding arrive in one frame (abi/protocols/9p.txt,
# docs/design-domains.md, "Floors from a protocol").
# A facet row's rights are its grant's (docs/design-files.md, "Building, binding and
# mounting"): ro reads, rw also writes and rx also runs what it reads, and no member
# both writes and runs (F6). A row that states none is rw. The grant refuses every
# writing operation of an ro or rx grant before its callback (F5), and nothing a client
# sends widens its rights. files_grant is the manifest check's acceptance of the
# parameter, whose kind alone bounds it to these members.
# A facet row the build derives (an app's, docs/design-packaging.md, "The apps row")
# takes the quantities below: requests of files_max_frame_min bytes, 16 in flight, and a
# grant process heap of 262144 words (2 MiB), which holds 16 write requests of 64 KiB.
service files 5
  parameter rights 1 member optional member files_rights checked_by files_grant
  quantity max_request 131072
  quantity in_flight 16
  quantity reductions 100000
  quantity period_ns 1000000000
  quantity max_heap_size 262144
limit files_max_frame_min u32 131072
enum files_rights u8
  value ro 1
  value rw 2
  value rx 3
